# First cycle: product evaluation drafts

2026-10-31 JST. Owner-selected dapp targets: Murakumo and Yataverse.
Prepared by Codex for the seven role profiles; these are not outputs from
running those bots. Customer identities, prices, measured capacity, live
orders and execution receipts remain unknown.

## Murakumo: one accepted inference outcome

Proposed first user: a small team that repeatedly turns its own approved,
non-sensitive notes into a factual briefing or an evaluation/proposal draft.
The trial package is one bounded task, one fixed model/artifact, a quality
rubric, a completion/latency limit and a resource/cost cap. It does not
promise a model, GPU backend or endpoint that has not been qualified.

Marketing prepares a description and demonstrable scope. Sales records
actual frequency/volume, data locality, user/approver, budget cap, support
and stop criteria. The adoption bot prepares the expected output and
checks; the operator selects a qualified release and supplies the input.
Local inference remains the default. Any separate inference-service
operation requires its own valid authority and, in a browser, fresh consent.

The outcome is the user's acceptance of the draft against the rubric.
Token throughput and model occupancy alone are insufficient. The node bot
records same-condition latency, successful jobs/GiB-hours and energy/job;
the economy bot links the accepted order/job/evidence to a distinct
accounting entry. No current measurement or paid outcome is asserted.

## Yataverse: one verified artifact round trip

Proposed first user: a team that needs to retain an accepted non-sensitive
artifact and retrieve exactly the same bytes later. The package declares
the artifact, rights/data classification, digest/CID, approved destinations,
retention promise if any, retrieval/restore criterion, size/cost limits and
support. Do not imply retention or independent durability from a CID alone.

Marketing explains exact-byte verification separately from storage custody.
Sales captures real artifact size, frequency, retention/recovery needs,
locality, budget and acceptance. The adoption bot prepares local hashing,
an independently approved write, readback/hash verification and a later
restore check as separate operations. This draft authorizes none of them.

The node bot records verified useful bytes, retained byte-time and delivery
separately from inference GiB-hours. The economy bot links evidence to the
correct service category, funding pool and payee; learning rewards are not
reused for storage without the existing economic gates. Upload and readback
are not, on their own, evidence of a funded reward or payment.

## Shared work-order fields

Each real trial needs: an actual requester and operator, product/release,
workflow/order ID, source/license/data boundary, requested result and
quality check, frequency/volume, deadline, locality/endpoint approvals,
resource/spend cap, support owner, cancellation/rollback rules, then job and
artifact identifiers, independent acceptance, accounting state and a
separate settlement state. Public records contain no customer payloads.

The steward keeps a board with these handoffs and explicit missing fields.
AWAI-108 retains the existing three first-use/two D30 continuation targets.
Internal marketing/sales tasks and external customer trials are separate
cohorts. Start dates are recorded only after the chosen release, host,
operator and measurement gates are satisfied.
